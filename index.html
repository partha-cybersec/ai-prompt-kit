Workplace AI Prompt Kit [YOUR ROLE]

Ready-to-use instructions and prompts for people who work with AI every day. Pick your role or task, fill in the highlighted blanks, and copy the result into ChatGPT or Claude.

How to use this kit
  1. Set up personalization once

    Choose your role under Personalization. Paste "About me" and "Response style" into your AI's settings. In ChatGPT, look for Personalization and custom instructions. In Claude, look for the personal preferences field in your profile settings, or a Project's instructions.

  2. Pick a prompt for the job

    Browse by role or by task, or search. Every prompt works on its own, and works better with personalization already set.

  3. Fill the blanks and copy

    Click "Fill in & copy". Type your details in the boxes. Yellow blanks turn green when filled. Copy and paste into a new chat.

Settings menu names can change between app versions. Never paste passwords, customer data, or confidential company information into an AI tool unless your company allows it.

Placeholders in square brackets are for you to replace. Text after "e.g." is only an example. Always review AI output before you use it at work.
